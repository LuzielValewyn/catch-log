    Del

    My name is Del, and this is the journal of my life, written in ripples and tight lines. Every species in this log represents a story of patience, of quiet dawns out on the open sea, and magical sunsets hidden along winding rivers. From the unpredictable currents of the saltwater to the serene depths of freshwater, each fish holds the memory of the exact day nature and I crossed paths. This isn't just a catch log — it is a map of my lifetime adventures.
